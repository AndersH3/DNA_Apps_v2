## Limitations and interpretation

- AI-generated code, comments, contracts, and documentation can contain errors,
  omissions, incorrect assumptions, or explanations that disagree with behavior.
- Tests and linting are evidence for the cases and properties checked, not a
  guarantee of correctness for every input or operating environment.
- Deal annotations express selected software contracts. No formal proof of
  the complete program was produced, and not every property is contracted.
- No real-world vendor dataset or clinical outcome was validated in this work.
  Biological accuracy, genome-build compatibility, strand orientation, and
  variant equivalence are not established by the checks.
- The genotype merge assumes compatible assemblies and strand orientations,
  compares genotype strings literally, omits missing/conflicted loci, and retains
  the last identifier at each locus. These choices can affect downstream results.
- The Y-marker merge reports opposite signs without resolving them; YSEQ
  extraction skips structurally valid unsigned alleles. Neither determines a
  biological truth from contradictory or absent evidence.
- Generated output is a data-processing result, not a medical interpretation or
  a claim of vendor validation. Independent review is needed to establish
  suitability for a particular scientific or operational use.
